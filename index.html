About me

Understanding systems.
Verifying correctness.

Hello! I'm Sipeng, a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, where I am advised by Prof. Lauren Pick.

My research interests lie in formal verification. I currently work on the verification of database systems, with a particular focus on database isolation levels.

Before starting my PhD, I studied CE at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, from 2020 to 2024.
